Greek and Mediterranean food in Pacific Beach since 1990

Looking for the best Mediterranean food in Pacific Beach?

Café Athena has served Greek and Mediterranean food on Garnet Ave in Pacific Beach since 1990. Dishes are made fresh daily from traditional recipes, from souvlaki and gyros to moussaka and handmade desserts, with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options throughout the menu.

Dishes to know

  • Gyros: seasoned beef and lamb sliced off the rotisserie, as a pita wrap with tzatziki or as a plate.
  • Souvlaki: marinated, char-broiled chicken or beef skewers.
  • Keftedes: baked beef meatballs with herbs and regional spices, topped with tomato-herb scorpio sauce.
  • Papoutsakia: Italian eggplant filled with seasoned ground lamb and tomato sauce, topped with feta and basil.
  • Moussaka: layers of eggplant, zucchini and seasoned ground lamb under béchamel.
  • Spreads: hummus, tzatziki and skordalia (a potato-garlic purée topped with grilled zucchini), with pita.
  • Horiatiki: the traditional Greek villager salad of cucumbers, tomatoes, peppers, feta, olives and red onion.
  • Baklava: walnuts, cinnamon and cloves between layers of filo, with a light rose-water syrup.

Why locals come back

  • More than 35 years in Pacific Beach, in Pacific Plaza on Garnet Ave.
  • A full bar with cocktails named after Greek gods, Greek wines, and ouzo.
  • Happy hour every day from 3 to 6 PM (dine-in), with small plates from $5.
  • Takeout, catering and group dining for parties of ten or more.
