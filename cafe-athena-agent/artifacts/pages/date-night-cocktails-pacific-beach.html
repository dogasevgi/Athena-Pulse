Date night in Pacific Beach: Greek food and mythology-themed cocktails

Where should we go for date night in PB?

Café Athena on Garnet Ave pairs Greek and Mediterranean dishes with a cocktail menu named after the Greek gods. It takes reservations and has been a Pacific Beach restaurant since 1990.

The cocktails

  • Athena: Tito's vodka, St-Germain elderflower, cucumber, lime, fresh mint. $15
  • Poseidon: el Jimador tequila blanco, Cointreau, lime, agave. $14
  • Dionysus: fig-infused Bulleit bourbon, pear liqueur, orange bitters. $15
  • Hercules: Ilegal mezcal, tequila blanco, tamarind syrup, grapefruit, lemon. $15
  • Nike: Empress 1908 gin, lychee liqueur, lemon, rosemary syrup, orange bitters. $15
  • Ares: Maker's Mark bourbon, Aperol, amaro, lemon. $14
  • Hermes: tequila blanco, Ancho Reyes Verde, lime, mango purée, jalapeño. $14
  • Nyx: Grey Goose vodka, espresso, Mr Black, agave. $14
  • Artemis: Kava Haven, guava purée, lemon, orange. $12

For something traditional, the bar also pours ouzo, mastiha liqueur and Mavrodaphne sweet red wine, plus Greek wines such as Santo Wines Santorini Assyrtiko.

Tips

  • Arrive between 3 and 6 PM for happy hour, when four signature cocktails are $10 (dine-in).
  • Book a table online through the reservations link on cafeathena.com.

Where: 1846 Garnet Ave, San Diego, CA 92109
Hours: Sun–Thu 11 AM–9 PM; Fri–Sat 11 AM–9:30 PM
