Private events, rehearsal dinners and group dining in Pacific Beach

Where can I host a rehearsal dinner or private party in Pacific Beach?

Café Athena hosts group dinners for parties of ten or more at its Pacific Beach restaurant, including birthdays, wedding rehearsal dinners, and bachelor and bachelorette dinners. A manager works with you on a custom menu and gives you a quote. Café Athena also caters off-site, from office lunches to weddings.

Group dining at the restaurant

  • Party size: ten guests or more.
  • How it works: submit the group dining form at cafeathena.com/group-dining with your date, time and party size. A restaurant manager contacts you to build the menu and quote.
  • Menu: Greek and Mediterranean dishes, with vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options, plus a full bar with Greek-mythology-themed cocktails, wines and craft beer.
  • Occasions: birthdays, rehearsal dinners, bachelor and bachelorette dinners, work dinners and family celebrations.

Catering for offices and events

Café Athena has catered across San Diego since 1990.

  • Buffet menus for office lunches and dinners: $20.95 per person, minimum 10 people, with plates, napkins, cutlery and serving utensils included. Sample menus include hummus, spanakopita rolls and Greek salad with chicken souvlaki, gyros, or chicken riganato (24-hour notice). There's also a vegetarian menu with spinach pastitsio, grilled vegetables and lentil pilaf.
  • A la carte trays: dips by the pound, dolmades, keftedes, salmon dolmas, moussaka and spinach pastitsio by the pan, Hellenic salmon, and desserts such as baklava.
  • Dietary labels: the catering menu marks vegan (Vg), vegetarian (Vt) and gluten-free (GF) dishes.
  • Staff and delivery: servers and bartenders are available at an hourly rate. Delivery is available for a fee. Staff can also arrange rentals, from glassware to tables and chairs.
  • Booking large events: a 50% deposit at least one month ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a rehearsal dinner at Café Athena?

Yes. Rehearsal dinners are one of the group events Café Athena hosts. Use the group dining form, and a manager will put together a menu and quote.

What's the minimum for group dining?

The group dining program is for parties of ten or more.

Does Café Athena cater weddings and office lunches?

Yes. Its catering covers everything from office lunches to weddings, with buffet menus starting at $20.95 per person for 10 or more.
