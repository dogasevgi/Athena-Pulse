Daily happy hour in Pacific Beach

Where's a good happy hour with real food in Pacific Beach?

Café Athena runs happy hour every day from 3 to 6 PM (dine-in only) at 1846 Garnet Ave in Pacific Beach. Greek and Mediterranean small plates start at $5, and mythology-named cocktails are $10.

Small plates

  • Hummos (vegan): chickpeas, sesame sauce, lemon juice and fresh garlic, with pita. $5
  • Tzatziki (vegetarian): kefir cheese and cucumber spread, with pita. $5
  • Yalanji (vegan, gluten-free): grape leaves filled with rice, tomatoes, mint and regional spices, served chilled. $5
  • Artichoke hearts (vegan, gluten-free): marinated in lemon juice, olive oil and garlic. $5.50
  • Spanakopita (vegetarian): filo pastry with spinach, feta, fresh basil and caramelized onion. $5.50
  • Feta bruschetta (vegetarian): feta, tomatoes, olive oil, balsamic, herbs and garlic on sourdough. $6
  • Keftedes (gluten-free): baked beef meatballs with herbs and regional spices, with scorpio sauce. $7
  • Rosemary potatoes (vegan, gluten-free): with red pepper aioli. $7
  • Falafel and tahini (vegan, gluten-free): chickpea and fava bean patties with herbs and spices. $7.50
  • Gyros: a side of gyros meat with warm pita and tzatziki. $9
  • Lemon herb veggies (vegan, gluten-free): fire-roasted, with skordalia and pita. $9
  • Calamari: with red pepper aioli. $12

Off the grill

  • Chicken souvlaki skewers (gluten-free): four mini skewers of marinated, char-broiled chicken tenderloin. $13
  • Souvlaki combo (gluten-free): two mini skewers each of chicken and beef. $18
  • Beef souvlaki skewers (gluten-free): four mini skewers of marinated, char-broiled beef tenderloin. $23

Drinks

  • Cocktails, $10: Poseidon's Margarita (tequila, Cointreau, lime, agave), Nyx's Espresso Martini (vodka, espresso, Mr Black, agave), Medusa's Mule (vodka, lime, bitters, ginger beer), Hades' Sour (bourbon, lemon, agave)
  • Wine: $2 off wines by the glass, $10 off selected bottles
  • Draft beer, $7: Modelo Especial, AleSmith .394
  • Bottled beer, $6: Mythos, Modelo Negra, Pacifico, Coors Light, Firestone Walker 805, Corona NA

Good to know

  • Happy hour is daily, 3 to 6 PM, for dine-in guests.
  • Many plates are vegan, vegetarian or gluten-free, so mixed groups can share.
  • Café Athena has been in Pacific Beach since 1990, in Pacific Plaza on Garnet Ave.
