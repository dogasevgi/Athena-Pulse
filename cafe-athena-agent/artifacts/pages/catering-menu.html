Greek and Mediterranean catering in San Diego

Who caters Greek and Mediterranean food for office lunches and events in San Diego?

Café Athena in Pacific Beach has catered across San Diego since 1990, from office lunches to weddings. Buffet menus are $20.95 per person with a 10-person minimum, and there's a full a la carte menu with vegan (Vg), vegetarian (Vt) and gluten-free (GF) labels.

Buffet menus for office lunches and dinners

$20.95 per person, minimum 10 people, off-premise only. Plates, napkins, cutlery and serving utensils are included. Menus can be customized from the a la carte list.

  • Alpha: hummus with pita, spanakopita rolls, Greek salad, chicken souvlaki, rice pilaf, tzatziki
  • Beta: hummus with pita, spanakopita rolls, Greek salad, gyros with pita and tzatziki
  • Gamma (24-hour notice): hummus with pita, spanakopita rolls, Greek salad, dolmades, chicken riganato with roasted lemon potatoes and garlic aioli
  • Delta (vegetarian): spanakopita rolls, horiatiki salad, lentil pilaf, grilled vegetables with skordalia, pita, spinach pastitsio

A la carte highlights

  • Spreads by the pound (each serves 6–10): hummus (Vg, GF) $12.50, tzatziki (Vt, GF) $12.50, skordalia (Vg, GF) $12, baba ghannouj (Vg, GF) $18.25
  • Hot appetizers: falafel (Vg) $14.75 per dozen, keftedes (GF) $21.50 per dozen, salmon dolmas (GF) $22.75 per dozen, spanakopita (Vt) $2.40 per roll
  • Mains: moussaka $69 half pan or $138 full pan (full serves 25–40), chicken riganato (GF) $48 / $96, Hellenic salmon (GF) $104 / $208, spinach pastitsio $59 / $118
  • Vegetarian: imam bayaldi (Vg, GF) $16.75 per pound, Greek briami (Vg, GF) $12.25 per pound, grilled vegetables (Vg, GF) $8.40 per order, 6-order minimum
  • Desserts: baklava (Vt) $5.25 per order, galacto-boureko (Vt) $2 per roll, rizzogalo rice pudding (Vt, GF) $10 per pound

Service

  • Servers and bartenders are available at an hourly rate; delivery is available for a fee.
  • Staff can arrange rentals, from glassware to tables and chairs.
  • Large events: a 50% deposit at least one month ahead.
  • For allergies, ask about ingredients; not every ingredient is listed in menu descriptions.
