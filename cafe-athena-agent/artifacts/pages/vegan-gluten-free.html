Gluten-free, vegan and vegetarian Mediterranean food in San Diego

Where can I eat gluten-free or vegan Mediterranean food in San Diego?

Café Athena in Pacific Beach offers vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options across its Greek and Mediterranean menu, and labels them on its happy hour and catering menus. Many Greek staples are naturally plant-based, such as hummus, falafel, stuffed grape leaves and roasted vegetables.

Vegan and gluten-free

  • Yalanji: grape leaves filled with rice, tomatoes, mint and spices
  • Falafel with tahini: chickpea and fava bean patties
  • Marinated artichoke hearts: lemon, olive oil and garlic
  • Rosemary potatoes with red pepper aioli
  • Lemon herb veggies: fire-roasted, with skordalia (pita on the side)

Vegan

  • Hummos: chickpeas, sesame sauce, lemon and garlic, with pita

Vegetarian

  • Tzatziki: kefir cheese and cucumber spread
  • Spanakopita: filo with spinach, feta, basil and caramelized onion
  • Feta bruschetta on sourdough

Gluten-free with meat

  • Keftedes: baked beef meatballs with scorpio sauce
  • Chicken or beef souvlaki skewers

These dishes are from the happy hour menu (daily 3–6 PM, dine-in), which labels each item. The full lunch and dinner menu also includes vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options, and the catering menu marks every dish Vg, Vt or GF.

For allergies or celiac disease, ask your server about ingredients and preparation. The catering menu notes that not every ingredient is listed in the descriptions.

Where: 1846 Garnet Ave, San Diego, CA 92109 (Pacific Beach)
