# About Café Athena

Café Athena has served Greek and Mediterranean food in Pacific Beach since 1990. More than 35 years later, it's still on Garnet Ave in Pacific Plaza, and still a neighborhood favorite for date nights, family dinners and catching up with friends.

Every dish is prepared fresh daily from traditional recipes, from souvlaki, gyros and moussaka to fresh salads and handmade desserts. The menu includes vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options throughout.

The full bar pours cocktails named after the Greek gods, including the Athena, Poseidon and Dionysus, alongside Greek and California wines and craft beer. Happy hour runs every day from 3 to 6 PM, with small plates and $10 signature cocktails for dine-in guests.

For bigger occasions, Café Athena hosts group dining for parties of ten or more, including birthdays, rehearsal dinners, and bachelor and bachelorette dinners. Its catering team serves everything from office lunches to weddings across San Diego.
